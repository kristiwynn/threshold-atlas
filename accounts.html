Accounts

In their own words, across 2,400 years

Every account here is one this site may lawfully reproduce. Where the text is out of copyright it is quoted at length. Where it is not, the account is described and the source is linked. Nothing on this page is invented, and nothing is copied from an archive that has not given permission.

Where the rest are

Archives this atlas links to rather than copies

Tens of thousands of first-person accounts exist. Most are held by two organisations whose contributors gave permission for their story to appear in one place and no other. That permission is theirs to give, not ours to assume, so this atlas sends you to them instead.

The gap

Why there are not five from every country

You are looking at ten places because those are the ones with an account that is free to quote or properly citable. Whole regions are missing, not because nothing happened there but because nobody published it in a form this site can use. There is no near-death prevalence figure this atlas can quote from Brazil, Russia, most of Africa, or the Arab world.

Two things change that, and only two. Researchers publish more work outside the West, which is slow and not ours to hurry. Or people add their own, which is why the form exists. Every account shared here is counted, kept separate from the research, and published with identifying details removed once a person has read it.
