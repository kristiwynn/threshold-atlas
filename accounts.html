Tens of thousands of accounts exist, most held by two organisations whose contributors gave permission for one place and no other. That permission is theirs to give, so this atlas sends you to them.

The gap

Why there are not five from every country

Six shelves, because those are the regions with an account free to quote or properly citable. Whole regions are missing, not because nothing happened there but because nobody published it in a usable form.

What is missing, and what would change it

There is no account here from the Islamic world, from Jewish tradition, from Africa or from the Americas. Both Islamic and Jewish teaching about what comes next is on the Beyond page; what is missing is a first-person account from either.

Two things change it: more research published outside the West, or people adding their own. That is why the form exists. Shared accounts are counted, kept separate from the research, and read by a person before publication.
