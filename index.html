Share of people who had an NDE, by who was asked

Bars show the published figure; the thin brackets show the 95% confidence interval for that sample size.

Two different questionsHospital studies ask survivors of cardiac arrest within days, so they count how often an NDE happens when someone actually comes close to death. Population surveys ask all adults whether they ever had one, so they count lifetime experiences across whatever brought people close.
Why hospital numbers varySix to eighteen percent is a wide range for the same event. Study design matters: how soon people were asked, whether sedation got in the way, and whether a strict scale was used to decide what counts.
